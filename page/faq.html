Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about iCryptos Dashboard.

What is iCryptos Dashboard?

iCryptos Dashboard is a free, client-side aggregator that curates and displays the latest crypto earning opportunities, including faucets, airdrops, mining platforms, and investment projects. It is powered by TTEarnCrypto and built by F9XR.

Is iCryptos Dashboard free?

Yes! The dashboard is completely free to use. There are no subscription fees, hidden charges, or premium tiers.

How do I bookmark a project?

Click the bookmark icon on any project card to save it to your personal Claim List. You can add notes and set a "Next Claim" date. Access your saved list by clicking the tasks button in the header.

How do I filter projects?

Use the filter button (sliders icon) next to the search bar to open the Filter modal. You can filter by token, wallet type, and category. You can also click on any tag chip to filter instantly.

Does iCryptos guarantee rewards?

No. iCryptos is an informational platform that aggregates publicly available data. We do not operate, endorse, or guarantee any third-party project. Always do your own research.

How is data updated?

Project data is fetched from multiple sources and updated regularly. You can use the refresh button to manually pull the latest content at any time.

How do I report a broken project?

Contact us via the Contact page or email tontufads@gmail.com. We appreciate your help keeping the dashboard accurate.
