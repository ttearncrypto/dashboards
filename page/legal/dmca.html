DMCA Notice & Takedown Policy

Last Updated: June 7, 2026

1. Copyright Compliance

F9XR / TTEarnCrypto respects the intellectual property rights of others. We respond to valid DMCA (Digital Millennium Copyright Act) takedown notices regarding content hosted on our platform.

2. Filing a DMCA Notice

If you believe that any content on iCryptos Dashboard infringes your copyright, please provide us with the following information in writing:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized agent.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
  3. Identification of the infringing material and its location on our site.
  4. Your contact information (name, address, email, phone).
  5. A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized.
  6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and you are authorized to act.

3. Submit a Notice

Send DMCA notices to:

4. Counter-Notice

If you believe your content was removed in error, you may file a counter-notice including:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the removed content.
  3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the content was removed in error.
  4. Your contact information and consent to jurisdiction.

5. Repeat Infringers

We reserve the right to terminate accounts or block access for repeat infringers.
