Cookie Policy

Last Updated: June 7, 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

iCryptos Dashboard uses minimal cookies and browser storage for:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic functionality (session management, UI state).
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your filter settings, bookmarks, and theme preferences.
  • Analytics Cookies: Anonymous usage data to help us improve the Service.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services embedded in the Dashboard may set their own cookies:

  • Google Fonts
  • Font Awesome CDN
  • Odysee (embedded video)
  • Minnit Chat

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect dashboard functionality.

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View stored cookies and delete them individually.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Block all cookies (with site functionality impact).

5. Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to save your preferences and bookmarked projects. This data stays on your device and can be cleared via browser settings at any time.

6. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy as needed. Check this page for the latest information.
