Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 7, 2026

1. Information We Collect

iCryptos Dashboard is a client-side application that does not collect personal information server-side. However, we may collect:

  • Local Storage Data: Bookmarked projects, filter preferences, and UI settings stored in your browser's localStorage.
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage data via GitHub Pages or third-party analytics if enabled.
  • Cookies: We use minimal cookies for functionality. See our Cookie Policy.

2. How We Use Information

  • To personalize your dashboard experience (saved filters, bookmarks).
  • To improve the Service based on aggregate usage patterns.
  • To communicate with you if you contact us via email.

3. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with third-party platforms including:

  • Google Fonts - for typography
  • Font Awesome - for icons
  • Odysee - for embedded video content
  • Minnit Chat - for community chat

Each third-party service operates under its own privacy policy.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Clear your localStorage data at any time via browser settings.
  • Disable cookies through your browser preferences.
  • Request information about data we may hold about you.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
