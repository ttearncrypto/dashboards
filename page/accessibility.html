Accessibility Statement

Last Updated: June 7, 2026

Our Commitment

F9XR / TTEarnCrypto is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We continuously work to improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards.

Measures to Support Accessibility

  • Semantic HTML structure with proper heading hierarchy.
  • ARIA labels on interactive elements (buttons, navigation, modals).
  • "Skip to main content" link for keyboard users.
  • Keyboard-navigable dropdown menus and modals.
  • Focus indicators on all interactive elements.
  • High contrast color combinations for readability.
  • prefers-reduced-motion support to disable animations.
  • Touch-friendly tap targets (minimum 44x44px).

Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. We aim to conform to WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards.

Known Limitations

Some third-party content and embedded services may not be fully accessible. These are outside our direct control but we strive to choose accessible providers.

Compatibility

The dashboard is designed to work with modern browsers and assistive technologies including screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver) and browser zoom tools.
