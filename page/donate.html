Donate

iCryptos is free, open source and ad light.

Every tool on this site runs in your browser with no backend, no tracking pixels and no paywall. If it saved you time or helped you earn, you can support development.

Donation rails are being finalised. Until then the fastest way to help is to open an issue or pull request on the public repository.

Ways to support the project

  • Open Source at GitHub: read the code, report a bug, or send a pull request at github.com/ttearncrypto/dashboards.
  • Spread the word: share the dashboard with a friend who is hunting airdrops or faucets.
  • Join the community: Telegram, Discord and Facebook Group links are all in the footer of every page.
  • Built by F9XR: the wider network lives at f9xr.org.

What donations go towards

  • Hosting and CDN costs for the dashboard feeds.
  • Domain and certificate renewals.
  • Time spent verifying new earning projects and airdrops.

Not a financial product

TTEarnCrypto and F9XR never take a cut of anything you earn, never ask for a wallet seed phrase, and never sell your data. Please read the Terms of Service and the Affiliate Disclosure.
