Is iCryptos really free?

Yes. Every dashboard runs entirely in your browser and is open source. There are no paid tiers, no subscriptions, and no hidden fees.

Do you ever ask for my keys or seed phrase?

Never. iCryptos is client-side only. No server ever sees your data. If any listing ever asks for a seed phrase, it belongs on the risk list, not your dashboard.

How do you verify an airdrop is legit?

Our team manually reviews every listing: team history, contract audits, community reputation and payout evidence. Anything suspicious is risk-flagged with the reason shown on the card.

Do I need the PC dashboard, or is mobile enough?

Both carry the same vetted data. The PC flagship adds heatmaps, converters and keyboard control; the mobile dashboard is built for claiming on the go.

How often are opportunities updated?

Daily. New opportunities are added and re-checked around the clock, and market data streams live from CoinGecko while the dashboard is open.

What does week one look like?

Open a dashboard, skim the vetted list, filter by network, and start with the low-risk rows. Join the Discord or Telegram if you want a second opinion before you claim.
