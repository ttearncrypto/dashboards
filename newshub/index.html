News Hub

Two layouts, one set of feeds.

The News Hub pulls public Blogger feeds and renders them as a fast reading list. Both layouts read from the same sources, so pick whichever one you like and switch whenever you want.

Sources

  • Crypto: the TTEarnCrypto crypto radar feed.
  • Finance, technology, politics and sport: the wider F9XR news network.

Reading on a phone?

The mobile dashboard and the Classic View both work well on small screens. The Horizontal View is built for wide screens.
